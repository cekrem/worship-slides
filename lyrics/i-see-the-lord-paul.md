# I see the Lord. Paul

## Verse 1

I see the Lord  
And He is seated on the throne  
The train of His robe  
Is filling the heavens  
I see the Lord  
And He is shining like the sun  
His eyes full of fire  
His voice like the waters  
Surrounding His throne  
Are thousands singing
