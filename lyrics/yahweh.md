# Yahweh

## Verse 1

Spirit of Jesus  
Living within us  
Never to fail or forsake  
Unending promise  
Heaven inside us  
Whispers the sound of your name

## Verse 2

Holy, holy is the Lord  
Worthy to be praised  
Yahweh

## Verse 3

Filled with your wonder  
Here I surrender  
Held in your mystery of grace  
Calling me closer  
Waking desire  
Coming alive in your name

## Verse 4

Holy, holy is the Lord  
Worthy to be praised  
Yahweh  
Fire rising in my soul  
All consuming flame  
Yahweh

## Verse 5

He who was and is to come  
Is the one who lives in us  
The Great I Am  
Yahweh
