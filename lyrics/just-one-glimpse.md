# Just one glimpse

## Verse 1

Just one glimpse of his glory.  
Just one touch of his hand.  
And I will never be the same,  
cause his spirit keeps flowing through my veins.

## Verse 2

We sing glory to the Father, glory to the Son.  
Holy Spirit, fill us up, until we are one.  
Like a fire burn inside us, consuming everything.  
And as we lift our hands in worship, we will never be the same.
